Real Estate FAQs

How do I choose the right real estate agent?

Start with local sales that match your home type and price range. Ask how each agent prices, markets, and negotiates, then read recent client reviews. Meet at least two agents before you decide.

What makes a great listing agent?

Look for steady sales in your area, clear communication, and strong negotiation. Awards help, but results on homes like yours matter more. Ask any agent for recent examples.

Which neighborhoods do you cover?

Claire is based in Hillcrest and works across Lakeview, Oak Park, Westbrook, Riverside, and Downtown. If you are looking somewhere else, ask and she will tell you if she can help.

What does a certified negotiator do for me?

A certified negotiator has trained in proven negotiation methods. Claire uses that training to push for a better price, cleaner terms, and fair repair requests.

How long does it take to sell a house?

It depends on price, condition, and the neighborhood. Claire reviews nearby sales and gives you a realistic range in your first meeting.

Does Claire work with first-time buyers?

Yes. She helps first-time buyers set a budget, understand how offers and escrow work, and avoid common mistakes. She also works with move-up buyers, sellers, and investors.
