Know the Numbers

What It Costs to Buy a Home

Before you tour a single home, it helps to know what you will pay beyond the price. Claire walks every buyer through the numbers early, so there are no surprises when you make an offer.

Down Payment and Loan Types

How much you need depends on the loan. Conventional loans often start with a smaller down payment than many buyers expect, and government-backed loans have their own requirements. A lender can tell you exactly what you qualify for, and Claire can introduce you to local lenders she trusts.

Closing Costs

Buyers also pay closing costs, which cover the lender, escrow and title services, and prepaid items like insurance and property taxes. They commonly add up to a few percent of the purchase price. Claire helps you estimate them up front and, where it makes sense, negotiate for the seller to cover part of them.

First-Time Buyer Help

If you are buying your first home, you may qualify for programs that offer down payment help or lower rates. Programs change and have strict rules, so ask a lender early.

Claire is a real estate agent, not a lender or a financial advisor. Talk with a licensed lender about your own situation.

Home Buyer FAQs

Do I need a buyer's agent to buy a house?

You are not required to have one, but a buyer's agent helps you price an offer against recent sales, read disclosures, and handle negotiations, which are the parts that cost buyers the most when they go wrong. They also find homes you might not see on your own, including some that sell privately.

How much does a buyer's agent cost?

Buyer agent pay is spelled out in a written agreement you sign before touring homes, and the amount is negotiable. Claire explains how she is paid and who pays before you start, so there are no surprises later.

How long does it take to buy a house?

Searching can take a few weeks or several months, depending on your budget and how specific your wish list is. Once your offer is accepted, escrow commonly takes about a month.

What is an off-market home, and how can I see one?

It is a home for sale that is not listed publicly. Agents hear about these through their networks. Tell Claire what you are looking for, and she will let you know when a match comes up.

Can I buy a new home before I sell my current one?

Often yes, but it takes planning. Options include a bridge loan, a home equity line of credit, or a contingent offer, and each has trade-offs. Claire works with you and your lender to choose the safest approach.

What makes an offer stand out when several buyers compete?

Price is only part of it. Sellers also look at your pre-approval strength, how much you can put down, flexibility on timing, and fewer contingencies. Claire helps you decide which of these you can offer without taking on too much risk.
