Know the Numbers

What It Costs to Sell a Home

Selling costs come out of your sale price, so it helps to see them early. Claire gives you a written estimate of your net proceeds before you list, based on your home and your mortgage balance.

Agent Commission and Closing Costs

Commission is negotiable, and Claire explains her fees and what they cover before you sign. Sellers also commonly pay escrow and title fees and transfer taxes, plus any agreed repair credits.

Transfer Taxes

Many cities and counties charge a transfer tax when a home sells, and the rate can vary with the price. Claire helps you see how these affect your bottom line.

Preparation Costs

Small repairs, cleaning, and staging can raise what buyers will pay, but not every project pays off. She tells you which ones are worth it and which to skip.

Claire is a real estate agent, not a tax or legal advisor. Speak with a tax professional about any taxes on your sale.

Home Seller FAQs

How do I know what my home is worth?

Start with the free home value request above, then ask Claire for a market analysis. An online estimate is a starting point. A comparison with recent nearby sales, adjusted for condition and upgrades, is more accurate.

What is the best time to sell a house?

Spring is often busy, but the best time depends on your goals and your local market. Claire can tell you what is happening in your area now.

Do I need to stage my home?

Not always, but clean, decluttered, and well-styled homes usually show better in photos and in person. Claire advises which rooms matter most, so you do not spend more than you need to.

How long will it take to sell my home?

It depends on price, condition, and the market. Once you have an accepted offer, escrow commonly takes about a month.

Should I fix things before I list?

Sometimes. Safety and major issues are worth handling, while cosmetic projects may not pay back. Claire will go through the home with you and tell you which are worth doing.

Can I sell without listing publicly?

In some cases, yes. Private sales and early marketing to select buyers can work, but a public listing usually brings more exposure. Claire explains the trade-offs for your home.
