About Claire

Meet Claire

Hillcrest native. Licensed Realtor. Community connector.

Experience. Local Knowledge. Integrity.

A Hillcrest Native,
Through and Through.

Claire Bennett grew up in Hillcrest, a few blocks from the office she runs today. She started her career in mortgage lending, where she learned how buyers qualify, how appraisals work, and how deals fall apart. Then she moved into real estate to build a business rooted in trust, local expertise, and old-fashioned hard work.

As founder of Bennett Home Group, Claire pairs deep market knowledge with modern marketing and a small, responsive team. Whether she is walking a first-time buyer through the process from pre-approval to closing, or guiding a longtime homeowner through a sale, she brings the same work ethic and the same care to every transaction.

Claire is a homeowner and a rental property owner herself, which helps her understand what her clients are going through in today's market. She stays in close contact from start to finish and goes beyond the standard playbook to get her clients the outcome they want.

Off the clock, she is raising her family in Hillcrest. You will find her on the ridge trail, at the Saturday market on Main, or organizing the next neighborhood event.
