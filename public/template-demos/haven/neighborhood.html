Local Knowledge

A Hillcrest Agent Who Lives Here Too

Hillcrest sits on a gentle rise above the valley, with tree-lined streets, a walkable Main Street, and trails along the ridge. Homes range from 1920s craftsman bungalows and colonials on the flats to newer modern homes with views higher up the hill.

Buyers come for the community feel and the short walk to coffee, shops, and the Saturday market. Sellers benefit from steady demand, since homes here rarely sit for long when they are priced and presented well.

Lifestyle

Living in Hillcrest

Main Street and the Market

Main Street is the heart of the neighborhood: independent coffee shops, a bookstore, a few good restaurants, and the Saturday farmers market that draws neighbors from all over town.

  • Morning coffee and bakeries within walking distance
  • Saturday farmers market, year round
  • Summer concerts and an autumn street fair

Parks, Trails, and Open Space

The ridge trail runs along the top of the hill with views across the valley, and Hillcrest Park has shaded lawns, a playground, and tennis courts. Most homes are a short walk from green space.

  • Ridge trail with valley views
  • Hillcrest Park with playground and courts
  • Quiet, tree-lined residential streets

Schools

Schools and Education

Hillcrest is served by a neighborhood public elementary school within walking distance of most homes, a middle school on the east side of the hill, and a public high school shared with nearby neighborhoods. Several private and faith-based schools are a short drive away.

School boundaries can change and can split a single street, so always confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the school district before you buy.

  • Public elementary, middle, and high schools serve the area
  • Private and faith-based options nearby
  • Claire can share district contacts and boundary maps

Hillcrest FAQs

What kinds of homes are in Hillcrest?

Mostly single-family homes: craftsman bungalows and colonials from the 1920s through the 1950s on the flats, and newer modern homes on the hillside. A few townhomes sit near Main Street.

How much does a home in Hillcrest cost?

Prices vary with size, condition, and location on the hill. Recent sales have ranged from the high $700s for smaller bungalows to over $2 million for view homes. Claire can give you current numbers for the streets you like.

Is Hillcrest walkable?

Much of it is. Homes on the flats are an easy walk to Main Street, the market, and the park. Higher up the hill, most people drive to Main but walk the ridge trail.

How fast do homes sell in Hillcrest?

Well-priced homes often sell within a few weeks, and the best ones can draw multiple offers in the first weekend. Overpriced homes sit, so pricing matters.

Do you work with buyers moving from out of town?

Yes. Claire offers video tours, detailed neighborhood guidance, and help lining up local lenders and inspectors, so you can buy with confidence from a distance.
