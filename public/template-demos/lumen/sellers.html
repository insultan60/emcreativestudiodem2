Get Market Ready

The Staging Checklist

Room by room, everything your home needs before the photographer arrives.

The Basics
  • Declutter every room and clear surfaces.
  • Deep clean the entire home.
  • Remove personal photos and items.
  • Replace dead bulbs and open the drapes.
Living Areas
  • Remove extra furniture to open the room.
  • Use lamps for warm, even light.
  • Style with a few coordinated accessories.
Kitchen & Baths
  • Clear countertops and organize cabinets.
  • Clean appliances inside and out.
  • Set out fresh, neutral towels.
  • Refresh caulk and grout.
Bedrooms
  • Edit closets to show off storage.
  • Use crisp, neutral bedding.
  • Store valuables securely.
Exterior
  • Power-wash siding and walkways.
  • Mow, edge and trim.
  • Repaint or refresh the front door.
  • Add potted plants by the entry.

Seller FAQ

Questions sellers ask most.

Every home and timeline is different. A short conversation is the fastest way to a clear plan.

When is the best time to sell?

Spring traditionally brings the most buyers, but less competition in other seasons can work in your favor. The best time is when the market and your plans line up, and Maya can show you exactly how your neighborhood is moving right now.

Should I make repairs before listing?

Usually a few targeted improvements, like paint, lighting and small fixes, return far more than major renovations. Maya will help you decide what is worth doing and what to leave alone.

How long will it take to sell?

Well-prepared, well-priced homes in our neighborhoods often go under contract within a few weeks. Closing typically follows 30 to 45 days later.

Can I sell if I’m already living somewhere else?

Absolutely. Maya regularly manages sales for owners who have relocated, coordinating staging, repairs, showings and signatures remotely.

What does it cost to sell?

Costs typically include brokerage compensation, title and escrow fees, transfer taxes and any agreed repairs. Maya provides a clear net sheet up front so you know what to expect.
