Overview

A hillside village with deep roots.

Hillcrest climbs gently above the river valley, a neighborhood of winding lanes, generous lots and homes built across a century: 1920s Craftsman bungalows, stately Colonials and a handful of crisp modern residences tucked onto the ridge.

Life here centers on Hillcrest Village, a few walkable blocks of cafes, a bakery, an independent bookshop and a Saturday farmers market. Mature oaks shade the sidewalks, neighbors know each other by name, and the trailheads at the top of the hill open onto miles of quiet walking paths.

Homes rarely stay on the market long. Buyers come for the character and the community, and many stay for decades, which is why Maya often hears about Hillcrest homes before they are ever listed.

Hillcrest FAQ

Good to know.

Thinking about a move to Hillcrest? Maya is happy to give you a personal tour of the neighborhood.

What is the typical price range in Hillcrest?

Most homes sell between about $1.2M and $3M, with larger estate properties on the ridge reaching $5M and above. The median sale price over the last year was about $2.1M.

How competitive is the market?

Very. Well-presented homes average around 18 days on market and often sell near or above asking. Having your financing ready and an agent with local relationships makes a real difference.

Are there homes that sell off-market?

Yes. A meaningful share of Hillcrest homes trade quietly between neighbors and agents before reaching the open market. Ask Maya to add you to her private alert list.

What is the commute like?

Downtown is about ten minutes by car, and a regional bus line runs along the bottom of the hill. Many residents also bike in along the river path.
