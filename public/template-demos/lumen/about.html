Meet Maya

Dedication. Expertise. Results.

Maya Collins

REALTOR® · Principal Advisor · Certified Residential Specialist

(555) 010‑0142 hello@example.com @collinsandco.homes

Three qualities set Maya apart: an unwavering dedication to her clients, a block‑by‑block knowledge of the local market, and the quiet efficiency to keep every deal moving. Whatever the circumstances, she gets it done.

Maya began her career on one of the region’s most respected residential teams, where she quickly became a top producer. In 2019 she founded Collins & Co. to offer something more personal: a small, senior team, a considered marketing approach and one point of contact from first call to closing day.

Her sharp negotiating and her talent for finding the best path to a client’s goal are the keys to her results. In escrow she treats every transaction with real attention to detail, and when things get complicated she finds solutions.

Whether you are buying your first home, selling a house your family has loved for decades, or relocating across the country, Maya offers a skilled and personal approach that makes the process feel calm, clear and successful.
