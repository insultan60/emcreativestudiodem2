Overview

Set high on one of Hillcrest's quietest ridgelines, this contemporary residence opens almost entirely to the outdoors. Walls of glass slide away to a limestone pool terrace, an outdoor kitchen and a lawn framed by mature olive trees.

Inside, white oak floors, plaster walls and soft northern light carry through a double-height living room, a chef's kitchen with a scullery and a primary suite with its own garden. A self-contained guest studio over the garage completes a home made equally for quiet weekdays and long summer evenings.
