01Neighborhood Overview

Hillside Calm, City Close

Hillcrest rises gently above the river valley on the north side of the metro region. Its winding streets are lined with mature oaks and a mix of mid-century ranches, renovated classics and some of the area’s most striking contemporary homes.

At its heart is the Hillcrest village: a few walkable blocks of cafés, a weekend farmers market, a bookshop and the community green. Trails connect the neighborhood to Hillcrest Park and the ridge overlook, and the rail station puts downtown about twelve minutes away.

04Schools & Education

Learning in Hillcrest

Hillcrest is served by the local public school district, with private and charter options nearby. Boundaries and ratings change, so always confirm assignments directly with the district.

Grades K–5

Elementary

A neighborhood elementary school within walking distance of most of Hillcrest, with before- and after-care programs.

Grades 6–8

Middle School

A district middle school a short bus ride away, known for its arts and STEM electives.

Grades 9–12

High School

A comprehensive public high school with advanced coursework, athletics and dual-enrollment options.

Alternatives

Private & Charter

Several independent, faith-based and charter schools within a fifteen-minute drive.

Know Before You Move

Common questions from buyers considering Hillcrest.

Very. Well-priced homes average about 19 days on market and often see multiple offers in spring. A strong pre-approval and a clear offer strategy matter here.
The village blocks are very walkable, and most homes are within a 10–15 minute walk of the green, the park or the rail station. Hillside streets are quieter and more car-oriented.
