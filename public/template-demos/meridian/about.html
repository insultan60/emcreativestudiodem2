Meet The Hartwell Team

Built on Discipline, Driven by Care

James Hartwell spent his early career running complex operations projects, where missed details cost real money. When he moved into real estate full time, he brought that same discipline with him: clear milestones, documented decisions and a single point of contact who always knows what happens next.

Today the team includes a buyer specialist, a listing and marketing lead and a market analyst, all working from one shared playbook. Whether you are buying your first condo, selling a family home or adding a rental to your portfolio, you get one relationship and a whole team behind it.
