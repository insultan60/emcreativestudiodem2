01Overview

About This Home

Set on a quiet ridge at the top of Hillcrest, this contemporary residence pairs walls of glass with warm oak and stone. The main level flows from a double-height great room to a chef’s kitchen and a covered terrace that overlooks the pool and the valley lights beyond. Upstairs, the primary suite feels like a private retreat with a spa bath, dressing room and sunrise views.

Every Hartwell listing comes with a full disclosure package, recent utility averages and a neighborhood report from our analyst team. Ask us for the pre-inspection summary and floor plans before your tour.
