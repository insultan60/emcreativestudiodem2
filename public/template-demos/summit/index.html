Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right real estate agent?

Look for local market experience, negotiation skill, and a clear process from consultation to closing. A good agent should also explain their pricing or search strategy before you commit to anything.

What does a real estate agent do when selling a home?

A listing agent prices the home using market data, positions it for the right buyers, markets the listing, and negotiates offers on the seller’s behalf through closing.

How long does it take to sell a home?

It depends on pricing, condition, and neighborhood demand, but a well-priced, well-presented home in a healthy market often receives offers within the first few weeks of listing.

What’s the difference between a Realtor® and a real estate agent?

A Realtor® is a licensed real estate agent who is also a member of the National Association of Realtors® and agrees to follow its code of ethics.

Do you charge for a home valuation?

No. Reyes Realty Group offers a free, no-obligation home valuation for any homeowner considering a sale, whether that’s next month or next year.

What should I look for in an agent for investment properties?

Look for an agent who understands cash flow, comps, and development potential, not just residential sales. Daniel’s commercial background supports a numbers-first evaluation of every deal.

Which neighborhoods does Reyes Realty Group serve?

We primarily serve Hillcrest, Lakeview, Oak Park, Riverside, Westbrook, and Downtown, and regularly help clients in the surrounding communities.

Can you help me buy and sell at the same time?

Yes. We map out timing, financing, and contingencies up front so your sale and purchase line up, with options like rent-backs or bridge financing when they make sense.
