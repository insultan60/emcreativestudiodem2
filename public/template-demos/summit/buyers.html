FAQ

Buyer Questions, Answered

How early should I contact a buyer's agent?

Ideally three to six months before you want to move. That gives us time to get your financing in order, learn the neighborhoods and be ready to act quickly when the right home comes up.

Does it cost anything to work with you as a buyer?

The consultation is free. Our fee is agreed in writing before you tour homes, and in many transactions the seller covers some or all of it. We will explain exactly how it works for your situation.

Can you help first-time buyers?

Absolutely. First-time buyers are a big part of our business. We walk you through down payment options, closing costs and every step of the process at your pace.

What if I need to sell my current home first?

We plan the timing up front. Depending on your situation, that may mean a contingent offer, a rent-back after your sale, or bridge financing so you can buy first.

Do you work with investors?

Yes. Daniel's commercial background means every duplex, small multifamily building or development lot gets a full numbers review, including rents, expenses and upside.

How competitive are offers right now?

It depends on the neighborhood and price point. Well-priced homes in Hillcrest and Oak Park often draw multiple offers, while other areas leave more room to negotiate. We will show you the current data before you write.
