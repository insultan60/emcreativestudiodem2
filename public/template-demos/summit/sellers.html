FAQ

Seller Questions, Answered

How do you decide on a list price?

We combine recent comparable sales, active competition and your home's condition and features. You will see every comp we use and the reasoning behind the number before we list.

Should I renovate before selling?

Usually not a full renovation. Small, targeted updates such as paint, lighting, landscaping and minor repairs tend to return the most. We will tell you which ones are worth it for your home.

How long does it take to sell?

A well-priced, well-presented home in a healthy market often receives offers within the first few weeks. Our listings average 19 days on market.

What does it cost to sell my home?

Expect agent commission, title and escrow fees, transfer taxes where applicable, and any agreed repairs or credits. We will give you a net sheet up front so there are no surprises.

Can I sell and buy at the same time?

Yes. We plan the timing so your sale and purchase line up, using options like rent-backs, contingent offers or bridge financing when they make sense.

Is the online home value estimate accurate?

It is a starting point. Automated estimates can miss upgrades, views and condition. Request a valuation below and Daniel will follow up with a detailed, personal analysis.
