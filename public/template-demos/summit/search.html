Buyer Questions

Buying a Home: FAQ

How much do I need for a down payment?

Some loan programs allow as little as 3% to 3.5% down. A larger down payment lowers your monthly payment and can make your offer more competitive. Use the mortgage calculator on any listing to compare scenarios.

Should I get pre-approved before I start touring homes?

Yes. A pre-approval letter shows sellers you can afford the home, and most sellers in competitive neighborhoods expect one with your offer.

What closing costs should I expect when I buy?

Plan for roughly 2% to 5% of the purchase price. This typically covers lender fees, title and escrow, an appraisal, and prepaid taxes and insurance.

How long does it take to buy a home?

Most purchases close 30 to 45 days after the seller accepts your offer. Cash buyers can often close faster.

How does a buyer's agent get paid?

The fee is negotiable and agreed in writing before you tour homes. In many transactions the seller offers to cover some or all of it.

Can I see homes that are not listed here?

Yes. Coming-soon and off-market opportunities come up regularly. Tell us what you are looking for and we will reach out when something fits.
