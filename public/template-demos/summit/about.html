Our Story

Get to Know Daniel Reyes

“My job is to make the decision feel simple, not the market.”

Daniel started his career underwriting commercial deals, where every recommendation had to stand up to a spreadsheet. That habit stuck. Before he advises a client on price, he looks at the comps, the condition, the timing and the true cost of every option on the table.

He moved into residential real estate after helping friends buy their first home and realizing how few people had someone in their corner who would explain the trade-offs plainly.

Reyes Realty Group grew out of that idea. Today the team helps families buy their first and forever homes, sellers position properties to stand out, and investors evaluate duplexes, small multifamily buildings and development lots with a numbers-first lens.

Clients describe Daniel as calm under pressure and honest to a fault. He’ll tell you when a home is worth stretching for, and he’ll tell you when it isn’t. Most of the team’s business comes from repeat clients and their referrals, which is the measure that matters most to him.

Outside of work you’ll find Daniel on the trails around Lakeview, coaching youth soccer, or testing a new coffee spot downtown.
