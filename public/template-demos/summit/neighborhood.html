Overview

Hillcrest at a Glance

Hillcrest rises gently above the valley on the city’s north-west edge. Its streets were laid out to follow the hills, so many homes sit on wide, private lots with mature oaks and long views toward the lake.

The housing mix ranges from restored mid-century ranches to newer modern builds and a handful of gated estates. Buyers come for the space and the calm; they stay for the sense of community, the trails and the easy drive into town.

Lifestyle

Local Places & Daily Rhythm

What a typical week looks like when you live in Hillcrest.

Dining

A small village of cafés, a neighborhood bakery and a few chef-driven dinner spots along Crest Avenue, with more options a short drive away downtown.

Shopping & Services

A weekend farmers market, an independent grocer and everyday services within a few minutes, plus larger retail in neighboring Westbrook.

Parks & Recreation

Ridge-top trails, a hilltop park with city views and quick access to the lake for paddling and long weekend walks.

Community

Summer concerts in the park, an active neighborhood association and a holiday lights tour that draws visitors from across town.

Schools

Education in Hillcrest

Hillcrest is served by the local public school district, with several private and specialty options nearby.

Elementary

Neighborhood elementary schools within walking or short driving distance of most streets, with before- and after-school programs.

Middle & High School

Students typically attend district middle and high schools a short drive away, offering arts, athletics and advanced coursework.

Private & Specialty

Independent, faith-based and language-immersion schools are located in and around neighboring communities.

School assignments depend on the specific address and can change. Always confirm boundaries and enrollment directly with the school district.

FAQ

Hillcrest Real Estate FAQ

What is the median home price in Hillcrest?

Over the past twelve months the median sale price in Hillcrest has been about $1.95 million, with larger view estates selling well above $3 million. Ask us for a current, street-level breakdown.

What types of homes are in Hillcrest?

Mostly single-family homes on generous lots: mid-century ranches, newer modern builds and a handful of gated estates. Condos and townhomes are rare in the neighborhood itself.

Is Hillcrest a good place for families?

Many families choose Hillcrest for its quiet streets, larger yards and access to parks and trails. School assignments vary by address, so we always recommend confirming boundaries with the district.

How competitive is the Hillcrest market?

Well-priced homes typically sell in under three weeks, and turnkey homes with views often see multiple offers. Homes needing work tend to give buyers more room to negotiate.

What should I check before buying a hillside home?

Look at drainage, retaining walls, foundation and slope stability, and confirm that past additions were permitted. We can refer trusted inspectors and engineers.
